Internship Highlight: Guardforce Hong Kong

26 October 2023 Written by Franco Ng

In July 2023, we successfully facilitated a connection between one of our students and Guardforce Hong Kong, placing him in a Business Development internship role. This was a pivotal moment that underscored the power of mentorship and direct industry exposure.

Through hard work, dedication, and the skills honed from our program, this student thrived in the position. His journey exemplifies the mission of Invent Your Own Future—to create tangible opportunities that lead to better futures.
