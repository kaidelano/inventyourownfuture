Seminar with Olympic athlete Zachary Yuen

14 May 2024 Written by Preston Chan

Breaking Barriers: Lessons from Olympic Ice Hockey Player Zachary Yuen

When it comes to sports, success isn’t just measured by wins and losses—it’s about breaking barriers, inspiring others, and leaving a lasting impact. At our recent seminar, we had the privilege of hosting Zachary Yuen, a trailblazer in professional ice hockey. As the first defenseman of Chinese descent to be drafted in the NHL Entry Draft and a key player in the 2022 Winter Olympics, Zachary’s story is one of perseverance, cultural pride, and unwavering commitment to excellence.

Overcoming Cultural and Industry Barriers

Zachary’s journey to professional hockey wasn’t just about making it to the top—it was about paving the way for others. Growing up, he often found himself in spaces where he was one of the few, if not the only, Asian players on the ice. Rather than seeing this as a disadvantage, he used it as motivation to prove that talent and work ethic know no racial or cultural boundaries. He spoke about the importance of representation in sports and how visibility can inspire future generations to break into industries where they may be underrepresented.

The Importance of Adaptability in a Fast-Changing Game

In hockey—and in life—things don’t always go as planned. Zachary emphasized the need for adaptability, whether it’s adjusting to a new team, learning from failure, or handling unexpected challenges. He shared personal experiences of being traded, moving between leagues, and playing on the international stage, all of which required him to remain flexible and embrace change. His advice to young athletes? Don’t fear transitions—use them as opportunities to grow.

Discipline Beyond the Ice

Many people think professional athletes rely solely on natural talent, but Zachary made it clear that discipline is the true game-changer. He highlighted how his daily routines, from rigorous training schedules to strict nutrition and recovery practices, played a crucial role in his longevity as an athlete. More importantly, he stressed that discipline isn’t just about sports—it’s a mindset that can be applied to any career or goal in life.

Giving Back and Inspiring the Next Generation

Despite his many accomplishments, Zachary remains passionate about giving back to the community. He spoke about the responsibility that comes with success and how mentorship can make a huge difference in a young athlete’s career. He encouraged attendees to seek guidance from mentors, stay connected to their roots, and, most importantly, give back when they reach a position of influence.

A New Perspective on Success

Zachary Yuen’s seminar wasn’t just a talk about hockey—it was a conversation about resilience, leadership, and the impact of representation. His story reminds us that success isn’t just about personal achievement; it’s about lifting others up, embracing change, and staying true to one’s passion. Whether you’re an aspiring athlete or pursuing a different path, his journey offers lessons that can inspire anyone to push beyond limits and redefine success on their own terms.
