Past programme

Fintech & Investment Banking

27–31 July 2026 · 9 a.m.–5 p.m. · Hong Kong PolyU

A five-day intensive bootcamp for upper-secondary students covering live market data, financial models and investment strategy.

Programme details

Students progressed from financial foundations and risk assessment into modelling, valuations, derivatives and hedging, culminating in a capstone showcase.

The programme was guided by Dr. Allen Tai Hoi-Lun of PolyU’s Department of Applied Mathematics and Wilson Lee, Co-Head of Asia Equity Derivatives at SG Securities.

Past programme

Swift App Development

6–10 July 2026 · 9 a.m.–5 p.m. · Hong Kong PolyU

A five-day intensive bootcamp where younger students learned to code in Swift, design applications and pitch their creations.

Programme details

The programme moved from programming foundations through logic, modules and app refinement to a final capstone showcase.

Guided by PolyU instructor and AI and EdTech system architect Leo Lui Shing Fai, students explored SwiftUI, prototyping, UI/UX, testing and presentation.

Past programme

AI Innovation in Healthcare & Neuroscience

27 June 2026 · 4–5 p.m. · PolyU

A student conversation with leaders across medicine, pharmaceutical leadership and health-tech entrepreneurship.

Programme details

Moderated by Preston Chan and Jacky Kwan, the panel featured Dr. Chan Chi Kwok, Tony Cheng, Dr. Eric Chu and Bikie Wong.

The session explored how AI is reshaping medicine and what this means for students considering futures in healthcare, science or technology.
