About the programme

Eligibility
Hong Kong secondary school student in year 4 or above.
Responsibilities
Members of our student ambassador team will perform a wide range of tasks including outreaching to speakers, organizing events, and coordinating partnerships with schools and other peer groups.
Benefits
You will receive opportunities to network with post-secondary and industry professionals, gain access to exclusive events, and accelerate your education and career growth.

Ambassador Executive Committee

Three student leaders run the Student Ambassador team. They bring in speakers, organise events and build partnerships with schools and other student groups.

  • President

    LI Kwan To, Kelvin

    Leads the ambassador team and works with IYOF on its events and programmes.

  • Internal Vice President

    Muhamad Kai Delano

    Coordinates the ambassador team and the organisation of events.

  • External Vice President

    Lam Wing Ming

    Reaches out to speakers and builds partnerships with schools and peer groups.
