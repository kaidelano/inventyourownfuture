## Variant: Impact Mosaic

### Design stance
A bold story-led homepage that leads with real experiences, student activity, and programme outcomes.

### Key choices
- Large modular hero mosaic
- Programme photography dominates the visual hierarchy
- Outcome stories instead of conventional initiative cards
- Four-step student journey

### Trade-offs
- Strong at emotion, differentiation, and showing real work
- Less efficient for visitors who arrive with a specific task

### Best for
A campaign-style NGO homepage focused on impact and storytelling.
