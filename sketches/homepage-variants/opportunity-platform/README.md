## Variant: Opportunity Platform

### Design stance
An energetic, app-inspired homepage that puts opportunity discovery and student actions first.

### Key choices
- Dark hero with search interaction
- Opportunity filters and save-button states
- Dashboard preview to foreshadow the future app
- Compact, action-oriented content hierarchy

### Trade-offs
- Strong at student engagement and product continuity
- Can feel less formal to parents, schools, and institutional partners

### Best for
A website that increasingly becomes the public entry point to the student platform.
