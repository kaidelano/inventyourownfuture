## Variant: Editorial Trust

### Design stance
A warm, spacious, photography-led homepage that makes IYOF feel credible to students, parents, schools, and partners.

### Key choices
- Split hero with editorial typography and organic image shape
- Restrained use of all four logo colours
- Four initiative cards as the main discovery mechanism
- Founder quote as the final emotional anchor

### Trade-offs
- Strong at trust, readability, and institutional polish
- Less immediately app-like than the Opportunity Platform direction

### Best for
The public website as IYOF's long-term front door.
