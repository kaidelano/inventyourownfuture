## Variant: Initiatives-First Platform Hybrid

**Status:** Approved by the owner and team as the production homepage direction on 31 August 2026.

### Design stance
Combine the action-oriented structure of Opportunity Platform with the bold yellow, purple, teal, and pink visual language of Impact Mosaic.

### Content priority
1. Explain IYOF and its mission
2. Present the four initiatives as the main operating model
3. Show programme delivery and student outcomes
4. Invite school partnerships and show verified school-community reach
5. Show how the Board of Advisors strengthens IYOF
6. Preview job/opportunity-style posts as a secondary access layer
7. Give shareholders a clear picture of how the organisation fits together

### Key choices
- Yellow and purple modular hero inspired by the IYOF logo
- Compact, viewport-aware hero designed to read at 100% browser zoom
- Platform-style navigation, cards, filters, and save-button states
- Four large initiative cards before any job-post content
- Programme stories as evidence of delivery
- Two left-to-right logo marquees: universities/institutional partners above, primary and secondary schools below
- School attendees remain clearly distinguished from formal institutional partners
- Compact five-card Board of Advisors section focused on how each advisor supports IYOF
- Compact opportunity cards that resemble job posts without making the homepage a job board


### Trade-offs
- Strong at balancing student usability with institutional storytelling
- Denser than Editorial Trust and less campaign-like than Impact Mosaic
- Requires careful content curation so opportunities do not overpower the organisation narrative

### Best for
The approved production direction: a public NGO homepage that explains the company to students, schools, shareholders and partners while previewing the future student opportunity platform.
